ABOUT_THE_ARCHIVE

NEORUINS Chronicles is a fictional archive — fragments of media, artifacts, and transmissions presented as if recovered from a forgotten future.

It lives across stories, visuals, and objects. Some pieces you read. Some you watch. Some you hold. All of them are part of the same buried signal.

New entries are recovered over time. The archive grows the way ruins do — slowly, and all at once.
